# Use Cases

Load this reference for prompt types that need domain-specific structure.

## Photorealistic Images

Prompt as if a real photo is being captured in the moment. Say "photorealistic" directly when realism is required. Use camera language for framing and character, not exact physical simulation.

Include visible imperfections when naturalism matters: pores, wrinkles, fabric wear, oil sheen, worn surfaces, uneven street light, motion blur, water droplets, or ordinary environmental clutter.

Avoid studio-polish terms if the image should feel candid or documentary.

## Product And Catalog

Define product geometry, material, label integrity, background, edge quality, padding, and whether it is a photograph, render, or flat graphic.

Control:

- One product or exact count.
- Label text and visibility.
- Contact shadow, reflection, and surface.
- Negative space for copy.
- No fake logos, extra props, or watermark.

## Advertising And Posters

Write as a creative brief:

- Brand or fictional brand.
- Audience.
- Campaign concept.
- Canvas and placement.
- Focal subject and visual hierarchy.
- Exact copy and typography.
- Thumbnail readability.

## Logos

Ask for an original mark with simple shapes, strong silhouette, balanced negative space, generous padding, flat colors, and scalability. Avoid gradients, mockups, excessive detail, and watermark.

## UI Mockups

Describe the product as if it exists. Focus on real interface hierarchy, spacing, practical states, readable labels, and plausible data. Avoid concept-art atmosphere unless the user asks for a promotional image.

Include:

- Product name.
- Screen type and aspect.
- Navigation, header, main content, secondary panels.
- Exact labels.
- Visual system.
- Constraints against fake logos and decorative clutter.

## Infographics And Diagrams

Define audience, learning objective, canvas, layout regions, labels, arrows, legend, values, and readability constraints. Do not ask the model to invent important facts.

For dense text or technical information, use a structured prompt and provide all required copy.

## Multi-Panel Comics

Specify panel count, order, equal/irregular layout, repeated-character invariants, action per panel, style, and whether captions or speech bubbles are allowed. Keep repeated character and environment details explicit.

## Historical, Scientific, Cultural, Or Technical Scenes

State accuracy requirements and visible period/technical details. Important facts should be independently verified outside the image model after generation.

## Transparent Or Cutout Assets

Request an isolated subject with a genuinely transparent background (alpha transparency), clean edges, and no painted checkerboard or solid backdrop. State whether shadows should remain only when relevant. For hair, fur, smoke, glass, or liquid, describe the needed fine or translucent edges.
